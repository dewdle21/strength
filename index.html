    The rules

    Level up when all three are true:

    1. Every rep looks clean and controlled.
    2. Top of the rep range on every set, with 2–3 reps left in the tank.
    3. You felt fine by the next session.

    Within a level, add reps first. Change one thing at a time. Never train to failure.

    Missed a session? Skip it and do the next one. Never double up.

    Rough day? One set of each, or drop a level. Showing up counts.
